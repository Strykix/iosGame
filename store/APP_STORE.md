## Notes pour la review Apple (en anglais)

```
StarBarge: Don't Pop is a one-touch arcade game. No account or login is required.

How to play: tap the title screen, then hold anywhere on screen to throttle up and release to fall. Watch the heat (left) and fuel (right) gauges.

In-app purchases: two non-consumable cosmetic packs, available in the Hangar (palette button on the title screen). They only unlock rocket skins and give no gameplay advantage. "Restore purchases" is in the Hangar.

Ads: Google AdMob rewarded ads only. They are never shown automatically: after a failed flight the player can choose "Continue (ad)" to resume once. On first launch the app shows Google's consent form (GDPR/UMP) and then the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Ad privacy choices can be changed in Hangar > Settings > Ad privacy settings.

Privacy policy is linked in the Hangar and at https://strykix.github.io/iosGame/privacy.html

StarBarge is an original parody: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or referencing any real company, product or person.
```
